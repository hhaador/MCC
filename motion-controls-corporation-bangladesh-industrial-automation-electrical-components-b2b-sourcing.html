Motion Controls Corporation (MCC): Industrial Automation, Electrical Components, and B2B Sourcing in Bangladesh

Published October 26, 2026 • Motion Controls Corporation

Choosing the right industrial automation equipment is only one part of building a reliable factory electrical system. Businesses also need dependable electrical components, suitable motor-control equipment, correctly specified power distribution systems, and a procurement partner that understands their requirements.

Motion Controls Corporation (MCC) supports businesses looking for industrial electrical products, automation-related equipment, component sourcing, and project procurement support in Bangladesh.

Through its website, mcontrolsco.com, MCC connects industrial electrical solutions with practical sourcing and business support for factories, commercial facilities, manufacturers, and system integrators.

What Does Motion Controls Corporation Do?

MCC focuses on several areas relevant to industrial automation and electrical infrastructure.

1. Industrial Automation Solutions

MCC supports enquiries involving PLCs, HMIs, SCADA solutions, and industrial automation requirements. These technologies can help businesses improve machine control, process visibility, and operational monitoring.

The appropriate configuration depends on the factory's existing equipment, production objectives, and engineering requirements.

2. Electrical Control Panels and Power Distribution Components

Reliable power distribution is essential for industrial facilities. MCC helps businesses source electrical protection equipment and components for their electrical infrastructure requirements.

Relevant product categories include:

  • Miniature circuit breakers (MCBs)
  • Moulded case circuit breakers (MCCBs)
  • Air circuit breakers (ACBs)
  • Contactors and control relays
  • Motor protection and overload protection devices
  • Low-voltage switchgear and related electrical components
  • Components used in electrical distribution and control systems

For businesses planning a new panel or upgrading an existing installation, the selection should be based on electrical ratings, fault levels, load requirements, protection coordination, and applicable standards.

3. Motor Control, VFDs, and Soft Starters

Motor control plays an important role in factories that operate pumps, fans, conveyors, compressors, and production machinery.

MCC supports enquiries for variable frequency drives (VFDs), starters, contactors, and overload protection equipment. These components can help businesses develop appropriate motor-control arrangements when correctly specified and installed.

For example, a factory considering a VFD upgrade should evaluate motor power, operating load, required speed range, and expected operating hours before choosing equipment.

4. Branded Electrical Component Sourcing

Industrial buyers frequently need specific models from established manufacturers. MCC supports sourcing enquiries for electrical components from brands such as ABB, Schneider Electric, Siemens, and Fuji, subject to the required model and current supply availability.

The objective is to help businesses identify suitable components, compare quotations, and source products that meet their technical and commercial requirements.

Buyers should confirm product authenticity, documentation, warranty coverage, and supplier authorization where applicable before placing an order.

5. B2B Procurement and Project Support

Industrial procurement can become complicated when projects require multiple components, different manufacturers, specific technical ratings, and coordinated delivery schedules.

MCC provides B2B enquiry and procurement support, including quotation coordination, supplier communication, and assistance in identifying suitable products for project requirements.

This approach is particularly relevant to panel builders, maintenance teams, electrical contractors, procurement departments, manufacturers, and industrial project buyers.

6. Business Technology and Workflow Improvement

MCC's website also presents business-management technology intended to support operational visibility and more organized commercial workflows.

Depending on the business requirements, inventory management systems (IMS), customer relationship management (CRM), and quotation workflows can help companies organize product information, customer enquiries, procurement activities, and follow-up processes.

Combining organized procurement with suitable electrical equipment can help businesses manage industrial requirements more efficiently.
